About

Made for the BYD Atto 1 Dynamic (2026, CKD Indonesia): estimates the energy, time, and cost of a charge, and how far a trip will drain the battery — math the car's own UI doesn't do. Assumptions: 30.08 kWh battery, 300 km NEDC, ~90% charging efficiency. Tariffs are editable and sourced from current official rates (tap ⓘ under each field).
